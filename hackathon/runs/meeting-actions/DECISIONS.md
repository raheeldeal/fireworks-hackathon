# Decisions log (meeting-actions)

- **Generator model:** glm-5p3 with `reasoning_effort: "low"`. Default call spends ~150 hidden reasoning tokens per request at $4.40/M; `low` gave 0 reasoning tokens in a smoke test and `none` is rejected ("thinking-only model"). This keeps 400 transcripts at roughly $1.2.
- **Label independence:** the gold labels are NOT model-written. Owners, tasks, dues, and distractors are sampled by `scripts/gen_data.py` from fixed banks; glm-5p3 only writes prose around them. glm-5p3 is also evaluated, so it may have a small familiarity advantage on its own prose. Flagged in RESULTS.md.
- **Due-date scoring:** exact match on a normalized string (lowercase, punctuation stripped, leading "by/on/before/until" removed). The writer is told to use the due phrase verbatim, so this is fair to all models.
- **Headline metric:** item F1 (micro). Precision and recall reported alongside, because the distractor design makes precision the interesting axis.
- **Split:** 400 generated, first 150 (by shuffled seed) are the locked test set, remaining 250 are train. Train is only used for few-shot examples and (if free) fine-tuning.
- **Kimi-k3:** 100 test rows max per protocol; its F1 is reported on that subset and marked as such.
- **Budget plan:** ~$1.2 generation, ~$0.8 six-model sweep, ~$0.6 interventions and routing, ~$0.2 variance. Stop at $3.00.
- **Generation length:** pilot of 3 came out at 500-585 words and ~$0.004 each ($1.55 for 400). Target changed to 300-450 words, max_tokens 1000. The 3 pilot rows (ids ma-0000..0002, same seed) are reused as-is.
- **Kimi-k3 rows:** budget math (generation ~$1.45 + glm-5p3 full test $0.35 + others $0.2) leaves ~$1 for kimi, interventions and variance. Kimi scored on 80 rows (~$0.6) unless generation comes in cheaper.
- **Routing is computed offline** from the cached per-row outputs of the small and large models on the same test set (no extra calls): if the small output is invalid or low-confidence, substitute the large model's row and add its cost and latency.
